# Voice profile: [Your team or brand]

Fill this out once, then point voice-tone-auditor and content-ux-reviewer at it.

## Who we are

One paragraph describing the team/brand, audience, and domain.

## What we sound like (three adjectives)

Pick three. Don't pick ten — it dilutes the signal.

- Example: *direct, substantive, dry*
- Example: *warm, specific, practical*

## What we don't sound like

Three anti-adjectives. These are often more useful than the positives.

- Not: cheerleadery, hedging, corporate-speak
- Not: academic, preachy, jargon-heavy

## Do

- Short sentences. Verbs do most of the work.
- Concrete nouns. Name the system, the feature, the person.
- Numbers over adjectives. "40% faster" beats "significantly faster".

## Don't

- Empty openers: "In today's fast-paced world...", "As we all know..."
- Weasel words: *arguably*, *essentially*, *really*, *very*
- Filler transitions: *moreover*, *furthermore*, *that being said*
- Emoji in professional communications (unless the channel norm requires them)

## Banned phrases

Teams that care about voice usually have 10–30 of these. Examples:

- "leverage" → say "use"
- "unlock value" → say what value
- "best-in-class" → say why
- "delve", "tapestry", "elevate", "seamless" (LLM tells)
